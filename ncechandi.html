Nalanda College of Engineering, Chandi

Nalanda College of Engineering (NCE), established in 2008 in Chandi, Nalanda district, Bihar, is a government engineering
institution affiliated with Bihar Engineering University (BEU), Patna and approved by AICTE.

It offers B.Tech programs in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Civil, Electrical & Electronics,
and Mechanical Engineering.The college emphasizes both theoretical learning and practical application, supported by
modern classrooms, labs, a library, and hostel facilities.

Admissions are based on JEE Main scores through BCECEB counseling. NCE also has a Training and Placement Cell that
organizes internships, workshops, and campus recruitment drives, helping students secure roles in IT, core engineering.

With its mission to provide quality technical education and foster innovation, NCE has become a key institution
contributing to Bihar’s growth in engineering and technology.
